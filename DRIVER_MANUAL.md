# Oasis United: Local Deliverer & Driver Manual

Welcome to the Oasis United delivery fleet! As a local deliverer, you are the backbone of your town's commerce. Your goal is to bridge the gap between local businesses and residents.

## 🎯 Your Main Objective
To provide a seamless shopping experience for your neighbors, we need comprehensive product listings for every store in your town. The more products we have online, the more orders you will receive.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Identify Local Partners
Visit the stores in your assigned area (Hardware stores, Restaurants, Groceries, Artisans). Explain that you are helping them get their inventory online at **Oasis United** to increase their reach.

### 2. Gather Product Data
Ask the store owner for a list of their top-selling products or their full inventory. You need:
- **Product Name**
- **Price**
- **Category** (e.g., Grocery, Tools, Menu Item)
- **Image URL** (Optional, but highly recommended)

### 3. Prepare the CSV File
Organize the data into a CSV (Comma Separated Values) file. The format should be:
`name, price, category, description, image_url`

### 4. Import Products to the Website
Log in to your Deliverer Dashboard on the Oasis United website and navigate to the **"Import Store Products"** section.
- Select the Business you are updating.
- Upload your CSV file.
- Review and confirm the import.

## 🚀 Why This Matters
Once the products are imported, they become instantly searchable and orderable by anyone in your town. 
- **Residents** get the convenience of local delivery.
- **Businesses** get more sales without managing a complex website.
- **You** get a steady stream of delivery requests from the stores you've onboarded.
